## Review notes

Record the owner's actual outcomes and constraints. Preferences remain preferences; they do not prove feasibility, value, or causality. Keep an unsupported aspiration visible but outside recurring execution until it has a plausible validation or driver path. Activity, output, a project, or a tool is not material progress without a named outcome, driver, constraint, or decision contribution.
