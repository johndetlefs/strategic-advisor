## Review queue

Use `None` when a decision supersedes no prior decision. A previous decision is historical input, not proof that its basis remains current. For a material goal review, name goal and path dispositions separately, their exact targets, evidence basis, authority, affected commitments, reversal or reopening trigger, next review, and closeout state. Use an owner-declared linked-detail record when that complete review would make this register unreadable.
