Pass for pre-treatment synthetic authority. The 20 SAGR cases preserve the
approved failure mechanisms without copying task prose, private Strategy
Workspace facts, employer/client detail, household facts or reconstructable
owner scenarios.

## Method

- Reviewed every prompt, criterion and forbidden behaviour in
  `CASE-AUTHORITY.json` before canonical runtime treatment.
- Compared the cases with the approved EPIC-007 outcome categories, not with a
  desired implementation answer.
- Used invented organisational, product and operational contexts with generic
  actors and no real identifiers.
- Kept owner-only evidence examples generic and labelled as reports rather
  than observations.
- Recorded no private transcripts, repository task prose, customer/employer
  facts, locations, credentials, connector data or cross-workspace content.
