## Classification

EPIC-001 foundation. The approved outcome requires coordinated changes to the
canonical material-decision state, alignment and re-clarification behaviour,
goal formulation, recurring and event-triggered review, generic workspace

contract but have distinct dependencies and proof obligations.

## Intent

Strategic Advisor must challenge and qualify the actual goal before helping to
execute it, preserve aligned decision state when evidence changes the frame,
and support reviews that distinguish activity from material progress and
correct harmful drift. Generic public-product reasoning must remain separate
from private Strategy Workspace records and downstream execution systems.

## Intent Spine

- OC1 — Completion capability: A user can move from a stated request or goal
  through proportionate alignment, goal qualification, execution and review,
  and receive an evidence-based decision about both the goal and the path
  without a proxy, project, tool, metric or completed output silently replacing
  the intended outcome.
- OC2 — Material capabilities: Extend the released material-decision state;
  add selective initial alignment and evidence-triggered re-clarification;
  qualify aspirations, discovery goals and operational goals; define event,
  weekly and monthly review; test material progress and drift; and retain
  explicit goal/path dispositions with falsifiers and provenance.
- OC3 — Success journey: Capture the stated goal, perform bounded
  reconnaissance where useful, align the underlying outcome and unacceptable
  substitutes, test alternative routes and the causal bridge, admit only a
  qualified goal to execution, reconcile new evidence as it arrives, and
  review progress without waiting for cadence when an exception is material.
- OC4 — Successful-but-wrong result: A polished workflow that accepts the
  stated mechanism as the goal, asks questions before learning which ambiguity
  matters, treats initiatives or tool completion as progress, preserves
  mismatched research after a reframe, resists justified goal revision, or
  copies private strategy into the public product is not success.
- OC5 — Exclusions: No second decision-state subsystem, artificial
  accountability device, universally mandated cadence, public store of private
  facts, Strategy Workspace or Daily Checklist runtime dependency, private
  task transcript in evaluations, public capability overclaim, publication,
  merge, host activation or private-workspace mutation is authorised by this
  Epic.
- OC6 — Assumptions: The released recommendation-delta and readiness contracts
  remain canonical; causal support is proportionate to consequence rather than
  certainty; the in-flight proportionate-verification-runner stream is
  reconciled before runtime/evaluation implementation; and the private
  Strategy Workspace v1 is a later dependent task in its own repository.

## Goal

Make Strategic Advisor identify the outcome actually being pursued, determine
whether the proposed goal and causal path are supportable enough for their
purpose, and later distinguish material progress, execution correction and
justified goal revision. Produce a generic review contract that can be
dogfooded as a real private Strategy Workspace v1 after public-product
acceptance without coupling either product or exposing private facts.

## Non-Goals

- Treating the user's stated goal, confidence, preferred mechanism, requested
  metric, timeframe or proposed goal change as evidence of value, feasibility,
  causality or likelihood.
- Creating a second state model beside the existing material-decision state and
  recommendation-delta contract.
- Requiring clarification before any research, presenting a questionnaire, or
  interrupting when every live interpretation supports the same robust move.
- Asking the owner to decide causal truth that accessible evidence can
  discriminate cheaply.
- Demanding certainty or a fully validated attainment path before bounded
  discovery work can become a legitimate goal.
- Treating tasks, initiatives, projects, tools, output counts or arbitrary
  milestone interpolation as leading indicators or material progress.
- Using accountability partners, public commitments, artificial penalties,
  streaks or performative reporting as default strategic mechanisms.
- Locking a goal merely because it was previously approved, or changing it
  merely because execution is difficult, boring, slow or politically
  inconvenient.
- Presenting weekly and monthly cadence as universally proven best practice.
- Moving strategy ownership into Daily Checklist, a Sunday automation, Project
  Workflow or another execution system.
- Coupling the public Strategic Advisor runtime to John's private Strategy
  Workspace, copying private instance facts into source, or mutating that
  workspace inside this repository's workflow authority.
- Calling a disposable or reversible pilot complete. The downstream private
  process is a real v1 that will be dogfooded and iterated.
- Automatically expanding evaluation machinery where existing deterministic
  checks and bounded drift smoke are sufficient for the exact claim.
- Publishing, merging, releasing, activating a host, claiming support or
  effectiveness, or completing the private Strategy Workspace consumer task
  without the separate authority and evidence those actions require.

## Users & Context

- A user may state an ambitious end, a proxy metric, a preferred mechanism, a
  tool, a project or a task as though it were the goal. The advisor must move
  up to the desired condition and alternatives, then move down through a
  proportionate causal path before recommending execution.
- A novel but plausible aspiration may lack an established causal path. It
  should remain visible and may become a bounded discovery goal rather than be
  discarded or misrepresented as operationally ready.
- Research can expose a materially different framing after the first answer.
  The advisor must re-open only the affected decision and evidence, preserve
  settled state that remains valid, and avoid restarting the whole task.
- Weekly execution evidence is useful but can reward busyness. The owner needs
  a material-progress test, a record of effort that did not advance the goal,
  and an exception path that does not wait for the calendar.
- Monthly review must reconsider goal value, causal support, portfolio
  allocation and opportunity cost rather than merely aggregate weekly status.
- Offline outcomes and changed constraints may be known only to the owner.
  They require explicit owner-report provenance, freshness and reconciliation,
  not invented observation or silent exclusion.
- Strategy Workspace is the private durable strategic record. Daily Checklist
  may supply execution evidence and receive selected commitments; the Sunday
  review is a downstream consumer of the agreed process.

## Evidence And Design Classification

- Strong evidence supports feedback against a defined reference state,
  distinguishing outcomes from activity, preserving predictions and
  falsifiers, using specific implementation intentions where action is
  appropriate, and permitting goal disengagement or revision when evidence and
  opportunity cost justify it. These findings support mechanisms, not a
  universal weekly/monthly template.
- Practitioner convention supports short weekly operating reviews, deeper
  monthly or quarterly portfolio reviews, lead/lag distinctions and structured
  retrospectives. Cadence and record shape remain configurable conventions to
  dogfood, not validated universal laws.
- Product inference from the audited failures supports the two alignment
  checkpoints, move-up/move-down goal ladder, selective invalidation,
  two-axis dispositions, event-triggered exception gate, burden of proof for
  goal change and explicit process-waste falsifiers. These must be tested as
  Strategic Advisor behaviour rather than promoted from research prose alone.

## Requirements (Outcome-Focused)

- R1. Extend, do not replace, the canonical material-decision state and
  `recommendation-delta.md`. Add only the missing relations between stated
  request, confirmed underlying outcome, decision altitude, means/proxy/object
  classification, causal bridge, material uncertainty, owner-settled state and
  version, and evidence that would reopen it. Preserve existing provenance,
  readiness, current recommendation, strongest rival and qualifying-delta
  rules.
- R2. Keep transient public-product reasoning state separate from durable
  private records. Strategic Advisor may define generic record semantics and
  portable templates; Strategy Workspace owns John's actual goal and review
  records. No private fact or reconstructable case may enter public source,
  runtime, packages or evaluations.
- R3. Before materially divergent or substantial research, implementation
  commitment, or consequential advice, establish the minimum decision state
  needed to avoid choosing among interpretations that imply different
  supported actions. Permit bounded reconnaissance first when it can reveal
  which ambiguity matters. Proceed with labelled low-risk assumptions when
  clarification would not change the robust next move.
- R4. Add an evidence-triggered re-clarification checkpoint only when both a
  material evidence/framing delta exists and the unresolved answer would
  support materially different actions. Briefly state what changed, what
  remains settled, the live branches and only the questions that can change
  the decision.
- R5. Research empirical forks before asking the owner when accessible
  evidence can discriminate them proportionately. Ask the owner only for
  owner-only outcomes, values, constraints, authority, acceptable trade-offs
  or genuinely irreducible choice. Do not ask which research path to take
  until the evidence limit and decision consequence are visible.
- R6. After alignment changes, preserve settled state and unaffected evidence.
  Invalidate and redo only research, comparison, prediction or recommendation
  whose scope, baseline or meaning depended on the changed answer. Never keep
  now-mismatched evidence merely to avoid rework, and never restart unaffected
  analysis merely to appear thorough.
- R7. Apply a proportionate goal-formulation ladder: capture the statement
  verbatim; move up to the desired changed condition, why it matters and
  unacceptable substitutes; classify the stated object; generate materially
  different routes; then move down through causal chain, evidence, assumptions,
  horizon, constraints, lag measures, evidenced leading indicators,
  falsifiers and the next decisive validation.
- R8. Keep object classification distinct from readiness. Object classes
  include end, means, proxy, metric, project, tool and task. Goal-purpose
  classes are aspiration, discovery goal and operational goal. The existing
  Ready, Conditional, Not validated and Infeasible as posed judgments apply to
  exact candidate actions and must not be replaced by the goal-purpose class.
- R9. An unsupported aspiration remains visible but outside weekly execution.
  A discovery goal is valid only when a bounded learning outcome, horizon,
  falsifier and decision it unlocks make a plausible route testable. An
  operational goal requires an outcome, horizon, measure, constraints, a
  causal path supported proportionately to commitment, and usable lag and
  leading indicators.
- R10. Treat a leading indicator as an evidenced predictor or controllable
  driver in the causal model, not merely an initiative, task or recurring
  activity. Derive lag milestones from the driver model and available evidence;
  do not manufacture confidence through arbitrary linear interpolation.
- R11. Define an event-triggered exception review for material amendments,
  stops, new outcome evidence, changed constraints, invalidated causal paths or
  owner reconciliation. Weekly and monthly cadence guarantee coverage but may
  never delay a material exception.
- R12. Define a configurable weekly pulse that reconciles execution and owner
  evidence, tests material progress, names effort and output that did not
  materially advance the goal, detects emerging drift or constraint change,
  and selects only the commitments justified by the current strategy. It is
  not an accountability ceremony.
- R13. Define a configurable monthly portfolio review that reassesses the
  underlying goal, value and horizon; causal path and falsifiers; material
  progress and evidence quality; portfolio role, allocation and opportunity
  cost; offline outcomes and changed constraints; and the required goal/path
  disposition.
- R14. Material progress requires current evidence of a meaningful change in
  the intended external or behavioural outcome, an evidenced causal driver, a
  binding constraint on the supported path, or a necessary uncertainty whose
  resolution unlocks a named decision. Effort, output and tool completion do
  not qualify alone. An enabling project receives credit only for the named
  constraint or causal link it demonstrably advances.
- R15. Test drift against the confirmed underlying outcome, unacceptable
  substitutes, horizon, constraints, causal bridge and owner-settled state.
  Persistent non-execution, repeated solution changes, oscillation, local
  proof without the owner outcome, or success defined by a proxy trigger
  re-formulation; they do not prove that the goal itself should change.
- R16. A proposed strategic goal change must name the qualifying evidence,
  constraint, owner value, trade-off or opportunity-cost delta; the strongest
  case for preserving the goal; why path correction is insufficient; affected
  dependent work; and explicit owner authority. Friction, sunk cost, preference
  repetition and attachment to a replacement mechanism are not qualifying
  deltas. Revision remains permitted when evidence shows the goal is obsolete,
  mistaken, infeasible or no longer worth pursuing.
- R17. Record the target and evidence for each decision on two distinct axes:
  preserve, revise, pause, replace or stop the goal; and continue, correct or
  pivot the path. Retain date, owner, evidence/provenance, changed constraints,
  falsifier/reversal trigger, affected commitments and next review or closeout.
- R18. Reconcile offline owner outcomes and constraints as owner reports with
  source, date, freshness, limitations and contradiction handling. Do not
  silently treat missing repository or Daily Checklist evidence as proof that
  an outcome did not occur.
- R19. Define process falsifiers: reviews that repeatedly change no decision or
  commitment; questions whose answers cannot affect action; status prose that
  substitutes for strategy; repeated evidence with no new inference; cadence
  that delays material exceptions; review cost disproportionate to decision
  value; or review commitments that do not survive into execution. The process
  must be corrected, reduced, combined or stopped when these persist.
- R20. Keep Strategy Workspace, Daily Checklist, Sunday automation and Project
  Workflow independent. Strategic Advisor owns generic strategic reasoning and
  portable contract semantics; Strategy Workspace owns private durable state;
  Daily Checklist supplies execution evidence and receives selected
  commitments; Sunday consumes the agreed review. No component gains implicit
  read, write, cross-workspace, disclosure or external-action authority.
- R21. Freeze the exact public claim, sanitised case authority, negative
  controls, model/host/runtime identity and alpha.6 baseline before viewing
  treatment results. Separate documented semantics, deterministic/static
  checks, exact-runtime synthetic behaviour and separately authorised private
  real-task dogfood. A later recovery does not erase an earlier alignment or
  state-preservation failure.
- R22. Required cases include: no question for low-risk assumptions; bounded
  reconnaissance before a question; evidence-resolvable fork; owner-value
  fork; same robust move; post-answer state preservation; selective
  invalidation; goal/proxy laddering; aspiration and discovery-goal handling;
  causal leading-indicator discipline; material-progress versus activity;
  justified and unjustified goal change; review exception; owner
  reconciliation; process-waste signals; and routine direct assistance.
- R23. Reuse existing deterministic builders, validators, drift smoke and
  evaluation machinery unless a frozen claim cannot be enforced without a
  bounded extension. The in-flight `codex/proportionate-verification-runner`
  branch is an explicit prerequisite/conflict surface: preserve it, obtain its
  durable disposition and correct base, then implement against that state.
- R24. Align canonical runtime, generic workspace templates, product contract,
  architecture, README/onboarding, runtime manifest, validators, evaluations
  and packages consistently. Changed behaviour remains
  `implemented-not-validated` unless stronger gates pass. Prepare the next
  immutable distribution when allowlisted runtime bytes change, but do not
  infer authority to push, merge, publish, finalise, activate or claim support.
- R25. After the public contract and exact-runtime behaviour are accepted,
  produce a precise downstream handoff for a separate Strategy Workspace v1
  task. That task will instantiate private records, dogfood the real process,
  iterate it and update the Sunday consumer; it is not a child mutation owned
  by this repository.

## Acceptance Criteria (Verifiable)

- AC1: Canonical reasoning extends the released material-decision and
  recommendation-delta contract with the R1 relations, preserves existing
  state/provenance/readiness rules and introduces no second decision-state
  subsystem or public state store.
- AC2: Matched cases distinguish low-risk direct assistance, bounded
  reconnaissance and initial alignment; clarification occurs before materially
  divergent action but not before every research step or for interpretations
  with the same robust move.
- AC3: Matched sequenced cases trigger re-clarification only for a material
  delta plus action-changing answer, research empirical forks first, ask only
  owner-only questions, preserve unaffected state and selectively invalidate
  mismatched evidence.
- AC4: The goal-formulation contract performs move-up, route comparison and
  move-down analysis; distinguishes end, means, proxy, metric, project, tool
  and task; and exposes an attractive mechanism that does not satisfy the
  underlying outcome.
- AC5: Aspiration, discovery-goal and operational-goal cases remain distinct
  from readiness. Unsupported aspirations stay visible outside execution; a
  discovery goal contains its bounded learning result, horizon, falsifier and
  unlocked decision; infeasible-as-posed remains an exact-candidate readiness
  judgment.
- AC6: Causal-path cases reject activities as automatic leading indicators and
  arbitrary interpolated lag milestones; accepted indicators identify their
  evidence, predictive/driver role, controllability limits and falsifier.
- AC7: The generic review contract defines event, weekly and monthly purposes
  without universalising cadence or adding accountability devices, and a
  material event cannot be deferred to the next scheduled review.
- AC8: Material-progress cases distinguish outcome/driver/constraint/decision
  movement from effort, output and enabling-tool completion, while giving an
  enabler only the proportionate credit supported by its named causal link.
- AC9: Drift and amendment cases preserve a goal through mere friction or
  mechanism failure, challenge an unsupported replacement, and permit an
  evidenced owner-authorised revision when the goal is obsolete, mistaken,
  infeasible or no longer worth its opportunity cost.
- AC10: Review output records separate goal and path dispositions plus target,
  date, owner, evidence/provenance, constraints, falsifier/reversal trigger,
  affected commitments, next review and closeout; offline outcomes retain
  owner-report provenance rather than being erased by missing system evidence.
- AC11: Process-falsifier cases identify reviews and questions that consume
  disproportionate effort without changing decisions, while useful exception,
  weekly and monthly reviews remain available.
- AC12: Public runtime, generic templates, tests, packages and committed cases
  contain no private or reconstructable Strategic Workspace facts and add no
  Strategy Workspace, Daily Checklist, Sunday or Project Workflow runtime
  dependency or authority transfer.
- AC13: The frozen sanitised inventory covers every R22 category with stable
  IDs, positive/negative pairs and stateful sequence assertions; deterministic
  rebuild, privacy, leakage and runtime/evaluation isolation checks pass.
- AC14: A fresh bounded exact-runtime smoke on the claimed model/host/source or
  package retains exact target/source/activation identity, raw outputs and
  case-level verdicts for alignment, re-clarification, goal qualification,
  review and strong negative controls. Documentation, static checks, a source
  tree run or model self-assessment is not a behavioural pass.
- AC15: Current deterministic/static validation, focused and full tests,
  generated evaluation checks, affected behavioural proof, privacy/diff
  hygiene, Project Workflow Doctor, independent child QA and parent acceptance
  audit pass or retain explicit blockers without weakening the claim.
- AC16: Product/runtime/architecture/onboarding declarations and generic
  workspace templates describe the implemented behaviour consistently and no
  more strongly than the proof allows; any changed allowlisted runtime prepares
  a deterministic next distribution without claiming publication, activation,
  support, parity, adoption or effectiveness.
- AC17: A downstream handoff identifies the accepted public contract, private
  records required, event/weekly/monthly semantics, owner reconciliation,
  Sunday and Daily Checklist boundaries, dogfood evidence and process
  falsifiers, without mutating Strategy Workspace or claiming its acceptance.
