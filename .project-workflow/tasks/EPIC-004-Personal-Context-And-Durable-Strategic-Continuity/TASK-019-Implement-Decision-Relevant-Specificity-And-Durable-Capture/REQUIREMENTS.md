## Goal

Make canonical Strategic Advisor behaviour preserve, retrieve, use, maintain,
correct, and supersede exact personal context whenever specificity can change a
decision, without reciting irrelevant details or converting context access into
unsupported specialist authority.

## Non-Goals

- Implementing workspace file mechanics owned by TASK-018.
- Producing exact-runtime behavioural evidence owned by TASK-020.
- Adding specialist medical, financial, legal, or relationship diagnosis or
  transaction capabilities.

## Users & Context

- Users expect recommendations to reflect their actual ages, relationships,
  amounts, histories, projects, goals, and constraints.
- Users also need irrelevant private facts left out of an answer when those
  facts do not change the causal account.

## Requirements (Outcome-Focused)

- R1. Canonical instructions define decision-relevant specificity as preserving
  the exact value of a supplied or stored detail when different values could
  materially change analysis or action.
- R2. The advisor asks only for missing specifics that could change the
  decision and never anonymises an authorised fact during normal workspace use.
- R3. Workspace retrieval follows causal relevance rather than a generic
  minimum-data or whole-person ceiling; irrelevant records are not loaded or
  gratuitously repeated.
- R4. Under standing authority, the advisor captures structured durable facts,
  objectives, constraints, claims, and decisions with provenance, freshness,
  limitations, and changelog evidence rather than storing raw transcripts by
  default.
- R5. Corrections preserve the prior record through explicit supersession or
  change history; deletion and cross-workspace disclosure remain explicit.
- R6. Personal, household, relationship, health, financial, and legal facts may
  shape any materially connected strategy. Consequence and competence control
  advice, not whether the fact may be considered.
