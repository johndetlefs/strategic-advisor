## Lessons

- Selective invocation, minimum-sufficient altitude, durable continuity, and
  option-search breadth are separate controls. Repository or workspace presence
  must not collapse them into one implicit mode.
- The portable workspace is most useful when first value requires no setup and
  durable continuity is introduced only after retained context can change a
  later decision.
- Progressive onboarding must show authority separation where continuity is
  introduced: retention does not grant reading, writing, disclosure, external
  action, integration, or cross-workspace authority.
- Exact personal Codex dogfood and public product evidence must remain separate;
  useful private work can inform later synthetic regressions without copying or
  reconstructing private records.
