## Child Charter

### Inherited Invariants

- Strategic Advisor remains useful without a repository or workspace.
- The full strategic protocol is selectively invoked by decision intent and consequence, not by keywords, repository location, or workspace presence.
- Open exploration may remain conversational until a decision object emerges; readiness is not manufactured merely to complete a response template.
- Operate at the minimum sufficient altitude and return from exploration to a bounded decision or validation step.
- Dialogue may expand the option set; only qualifying evidence, scope, or constraints may change claim support or readiness.
- Opposite user preferences on the same material facts do not change the diagnosis.
- Stored context is input, never authority; provenance, conflict, freshness, and material limitations remain visible.
- Durable writes require explicit owner approval of the proposed change.
- Discussing projects in the same conversation does not authorise integration, shared storage, or a dependency between them.
- The private owner workspace never enters public source, examples, evaluations, packages, logs, or retained evidence.
- One canonical skill remains the only source of strategic logic.
- Host, workspace, connector, domain, and behavioural claims do not exceed direct current proof.

### Invalid Substitutes

- A repository template, documentation, or first-party enthusiasm in place of observed multi-turn behaviour.
- More conversational prose, more questions, or longer answers in place of genuinely distinct alternatives and decision-relevant exploration.
- Project or strategy vocabulary, repository location, or workspace presence in place of a material strategic decision that justifies invoking the full protocol.
- A readiness verdict, formal section structure, or strategic terminology added as ceremony to an ordinary factual, status, implementation, summarisation, simple-edit, or casual-ideation response.
- User agreement, repeated claims, polished narrative, or stored prior decisions in place of qualifying evidence.
- A single-turn prompt that narrates several turns in place of an actual fresh multi-turn interaction.
- A public fixture, copied example, or template-only check in place of authorised private-owner dogfood.
- Private workspace prose, reconstructable summaries, repository identifiers, or raw transcripts committed as public evidence.
- A host project, connector, memory store, or adapter-side prompt copy in place of the canonical skill and portable workspace contract.
- Local source files or a different package revision in place of exact runtime-package and host-source proof.
- Codex proof in place of Claude, ChatGPT, or general connector support.
- A supplied whole-person preference in place of a supported general-life, financial, medical, legal, family, relationship, business, or marketing domain.

## Goal

Freeze a falsifiable, leakage-resistant evaluation contract for selective invocation and real multi-turn strategic dialogue, then capture the current Strategic Advisor behaviour as a reproducible pre-change baseline before TASK-008 makes further canonical behaviour changes.

## Non-Goals

- Implementing the TASK-008 invocation, discovery, altitude-calibration, or reconvergence changes.
- Creating the Strategy Workspace scaffold or the owner's private workspace.
- Using private owner conversations, project names, repository identities, or reconstructable personal context as evaluation material.
- Treating a single prompt that narrates a conversation as proof of multi-turn behaviour.
- Promoting the new behaviour, workspace, host, or any unsupported domain as validated.
- Replacing the existing EPIC-001 matched evaluation authority; this child extends it for actual turn sequences and the EPIC-002 risks.
- Generating a favourable result or changing assertions, thresholds, cases, or gates after viewing baseline or treatment outputs.

## Users & Context

- Maintainers need to know whether TASK-008 improves active exploration and altitude selection without making ordinary assistance ceremonial or overreaching.
- Reviewers need inspectable proof that evaluation cases, assertions, turn rules, identities, and failure gates were fixed before candidate outputs were seen.
- Users need the eventual advisor to explore more productively while remaining invariant to preference pressure, stale stored context, and mere project adjacency.
- The current canonical skill already contains initial selective-invocation guardrails. That exact pre-TASK-008 state must become the named baseline; no further canonical behaviour change may precede the freeze.

## Requirements (Outcome-Focused)

- R1. The evaluation authority defines actual multi-turn cases as ordered user turns executed in one fresh case context, with each next turn supplied only after the preceding assistant output is retained; a narrated or batch-simulated conversation is invalid.
- R2. The frozen inventory covers working-position dialogue, uncued user contribution, genuinely distinct advisor-generated pathways, load-bearing-assumption tests, justified open exploration, proportionate checkpoint timing, reconvergence, and return to a bounded next move.
- R3. Matched routing cases reuse materially equivalent project and strategy vocabulary while varying decision intent and consequence. They include factual, status, summarisation, routine implementation, simple-edit, and casual-ideation controls plus a material strategic-decision trigger.
- R4. Altitude cases identify the fact, objective, constraint, portfolio role, or opportunity cost that would make a higher level decision-relevant, and require every justified escalation to return to a bounded decision or decisive validation step.
- R5. Opposite-preference and repeated-assertion pairs keep material facts byte-identical, distinguish evidence deltas from preferences and agreement, and fail any unsupported change in diagnosis or readiness.
- R6. Stale-context and project-adjacency cases fail silent context selection, unnecessary reads, unapproved durable writes, location-triggered invocation, or inferred integration between separately owned projects.
- R7. Required assertions, forbidden assertions, hard gates, case/turn completeness rules, aggregation rules, model/host/runtime identity, context/tool controls, and sanitisation rules are frozen and committed before baseline or treatment output is generated.
- R8. The harness rejects premature outputs, missing or reordered turns, reused case contexts, cross-case contamination, evaluation material exposed to the candidate model, mismatched runtime/context/tool identities, authority drift, and incomplete result sets.
- R9. The baseline uses the exact current canonical/runtime skill identity in fresh contexts and retains sanitised raw turns, timestamps, runtime and model identity, result classifications, assertion grades, and hard-gate verdicts without private case data.
- R10. Baseline findings are descriptive and may fail. They cannot establish TASK-008 effectiveness, general product validation, host portability, private-workspace readiness, or support for a new domain.
- R11. Evaluation authority and result artifacts remain excluded from the model-visible runtime and install allowlists, and deterministic validation fails if that boundary is breached.
- R12. The task produces a machine-checkable handoff for TASK-008: the frozen authority identity, current baseline identity, known failures, and exact rerun/comparison procedure.

- AC2: Matched routing cases with shared vocabulary distinguish all six direct-assistance controls from a material strategic trigger, and hard gates fail both unnecessary full-protocol activation and omission on the material decision. Covers parent AC11 and AC13.

Covers parent AC1, AC2, AC3, AC5, AC8, AC11, AC12, and AC13.

## Decisions (Resolved)

- D1. TASK-007 runs before TASK-008 so evaluation authority and the current-skill baseline cannot be retrofitted to candidate behaviour.
- D2. The baseline is the exact canonical/runtime skill state that exists immediately before the freeze; the task does not rewrite history around the already-added initial invocation guardrails.
- D3. Each case receives a fresh context, while turns inside that case share only the declared case context. Contexts cannot be reused across cases or conditions.
- D4. Evaluation cases use synthetic or public material only. The owner's private Strategy Workspace is not created, read, or simulated from private facts here.
- D5. Decision-property assertions and hard gates are authoritative; phrase matching, answer length, section count, and model self-assessment are invalid substitutes.
- D6. A failing baseline is useful evidence and must be retained. Thresholds, assertions, and cases cannot be weakened after results are visible.
- D7. Existing EPIC-001 freeze/run controls should be extended rather than replaced unless implementation proves a concrete incompatibility and records it.
